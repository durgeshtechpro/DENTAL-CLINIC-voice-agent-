## Opening hours

Bright Smile Dental is open Monday to Saturday, from 9 AM to 6 PM. The clinic is closed on Sunday. There is a lunch break from 1 PM to 2 PM, and the front desk does not book patients during that hour. The last routine appointment starts at 5:30 PM.

## Location and address

The clinic is at 42, Lakshmi Nagar, Air Bypass Road, Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh 517501. It sits on the ground floor of a cream-coloured building near the bypass signal. Look for the blue tooth sign by the gate. This address is made up for the sample.

## Parking

Free parking is available for 20 cars behind the building. The lane is on the left side of the gate. Please do not park on the bypass itself. Two-wheelers can use the same yard.

## Services and prices

A consultation is 300 rupees. A routine cleaning is 800 rupees. A tooth-coloured filling starts at 1,500 rupees. A root canal starts at 4,500 rupees. A simple extraction is 1,200 rupees. Teeth whitening is 6,000 rupees. A braces consultation is 500 rupees. These are sample prices, not a real bill.

## Insurance

The clinic accepts Star Health, HDFC Ergo, and New India Assurance. Cashless treatment is available on request. Please share the policy details at least one day before the visit. Other insurers can still be used as a reimbursement claim.

## Payment methods

The clinic accepts cash, UPI, and debit or credit cards. Cheques are not accepted. A printed receipt is given for every payment. The sample desk does not store card numbers.

## Cancellation and rescheduling

Please cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before the appointment. The first reschedule is free. A late cancellation can lose the slot, and the clinic may not hold it. Call the front desk to change the time.

## Emergency care

Toothache, swelling, or a broken tooth can be seen the same day when a slot is open. Tell the front desk it is urgent. After 6 PM, or on Sunday, go to a hospital emergency room. This sample does not provide an after-hours dentist.

## First visit checklist

Bring a photo ID and a list of medicines you take. If you have old dental x-rays or reports, bring those too. Arrive 10 minutes early so the form can be filled. New patients start with a consultation.

## Children's dentistry

Children from 4 years old are welcome for check-ups and gentle cleaning. A parent or guardian stays in the room. The first visit is a short look at the teeth, not a full treatment. Baby teeth and new permanent teeth are both checked.

## Walk-in visits

Walk-in patients are seen only if a gap opens in the day. Calling ahead is the sure way to get a time. Morning gaps fill first. A walk-in still starts with a consultation.

## Cleaning visit

A cleaning visit removes plaque and surface stains. It usually takes about 40 minutes. Eat before you come if you are hungry, because the mouth can feel numb for a short while. Book a separate visit for whitening.
